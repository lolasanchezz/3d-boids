# 3d boids

boids but 3d

made w/ three.js - basic boids algorithim but with a third dimension!!!!!!!!
stole a picture of birds swooping and flying in the air and made a sprite sheet out of it.

also notes off a pentatonic scale play depending on the sizes of the flocks.
every 3 seconds the amount of birds in a flock plays a corresponding note. the bigger the flock, the deeper the note


made for my account @alphawolf5000
